BRIKSQUARE CARCIOFO - "ALIA XL" MULTI-EFFECT - 64 EFFECTS

Global controls (apply to every effect):
  P1 = parameter A   (also modulated by CV1)
  P2 = parameter B
  P3 = parameter C   (also modulated by CV2)
  P4 = dry / wet mix
  B1 = tap: next effect   |   hold: jump to the next bank of 8
  B2 = hold: FREEZE + full wet (infinite tail on delay/reverb/resonator types)
  Onboard LED = heartbeat (alive), flash on B1 step, blinks bank number on B2-less bank jump, solid while frozen.
