BRIKSQUARE CARCIOFO - "ALIA XL" MULTI-EFFECT - 72 EFFECTS (9 banks of 8)

Global controls (apply to every effect):
  P1 = parameter A   (also modulated by CV1)
  P2 = parameter B
  P3 = parameter C   (also modulated by CV2)
  P4 = dry / wet mix
  B1 = tap: next effect   |   hold: jump to the next bank of 8
  B2 = hold: FREEZE + full wet
  All outputs are volume-matched and soft-limited, so nothing can blast you.
